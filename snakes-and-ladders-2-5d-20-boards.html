    Both players start on square 1. Take turns rolling and moving forward. A six is just a six — no extra turn.

  2. Enjoy the little climbs

    Land at the foot of a ladder and go straight to the top. A lucky shortcut!

  3. Take the slides in stride

    Slide to the tail, or solve a haiku to stay on the head. Fern always takes the classic slide.

    You need an exact roll to reach 100. Roll too high? Stay put and try on your next turn.
